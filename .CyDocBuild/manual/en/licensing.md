## Overview {#overview}

CyRSAssistant by Cyberalien is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. Third-party components retain their original licenses. Full English texts and notices are distributed in LICENCES at the ZIP root; see SOURCES.txt and AssistantRuntime-Notices.txt there.

The product includes the ReShade integration, Dear ImGui, nlohmann/json, pinned Microsoft DXC and a frozen Python/PyInstaller companion. CyToolsCore, CyUEMCPSDK, provider CLIs and model weights are not bundled dependencies.
